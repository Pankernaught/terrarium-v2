# Marketplace is deferred until trust, moderation, legal, and shipping controls exist (milestone M13)

A plant/supply marketplace is a plausible monetization path and a plausible way to
sink the whole project. Master plan App. B (marketplace) is blunt about the
prerequisites: money changing hands between strangers needs trust and reputation,
content moderation, legal/tax handling, and — for live plants — shipping and
phytosanitary controls. Shipping a marketplace before those exist manufactures
liability faster than revenue.

**Decision.** The marketplace is **deferred to milestone M13** and is explicitly
**blocked on the prior existence of trust/reputation, moderation, legal, and shipping
controls.** It is not a launch feature, not an early-monetization lever, and nothing
before M13 may assume it.

**Consequences.** Monetization before M13 comes from other levers (e.g. the
free/premium split, master plan §14/C3, itself owner-gated before milestones M6/M7),
not from transactions. The community structure (ADR 0026) and any backend (ADR 0021)
are prerequisites, so this deferral is partly a natural consequence of theirs. Live-
plant logistics (shipping, phytosanitary rules) are called out now so they are
scoped as real work at M13, not discovered late.
