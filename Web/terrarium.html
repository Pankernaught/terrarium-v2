Plan before you plant

A step-by-step Planner walks every decision: container, layers, plants, hardscape — previewed at true scale before you touch soil.

  • ✓ Cross-section viewer at real proportions
  • ✓ Custom substrate mix with a porosity model

Know it’ll thrive

An Eco-Balance score tells you at a glance whether your plant selection makes a healthy ecosystem — and a per-plant Fit Score flags the odd one out, with the reason why.

  • ✓ Whole-build ecosystem score
  • ✓ Light, humidity, moisture, temp, pH & crowding, together

Assembly, step by step

Instructions are generated from your exact build — not generic advice. Drainage depth, charcoal, substrate, planting order, watering and sealing, in the right sequence.

  • ✓ Derived from your container & plant mix
  • ✓ The critical first two weeks, covered

Care that fits your build

Personalised reminders tuned to your container type and plant mix. Sealed, lidded and open containers each get their own watering and humidity guidance.

  • ✓ Built-in glossary — no jargon left unexplained
